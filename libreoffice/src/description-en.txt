CellStat adds statistical analysis to LibreOffice Calc: distributions, Fit Y by X (line fit, one-way ANOVA, contingency tables), multiple regression, decision trees, control charts, process capability, Gauge R&R and two-level designed experiments. Select your data, explore results in the CellStat panel, then export tables and charts to a new sheet. Requires Python 3 with NumPy and SciPy (scikit-learn for decision trees). MIT License.
